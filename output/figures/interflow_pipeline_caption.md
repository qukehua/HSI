**Figure X. Overview of InterFlow.** (a) A shared conditional Transformer encodes the interpolated motion together with scene, language, goal, object, and enhanced boundary-history conditions. A global branch predicts sparse pelvis/object anchors and phase features; the dense trajectories drive trajectory-aligned scene queries, while a human-object cross query supplies local geometry cues. The local branch fuses these signals to predict the Flow Matching velocity field and auxiliary phase/contact/completion outputs. (b) The enhanced window-bridging token combines a motion-state encoding of past clean boundary frames with a human-scene interaction embedding obtained by cross-attending past body points to scene points sampled online from the local occupancy crop. (c) At inference, deterministic Euler ODE integration generates each window, fixes the first two prefix frames for the next window, and uses the completion head for segment-level stopping before SMPL-X conversion.
